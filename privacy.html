Privacy Policy

TripChoice respects your privacy and collects only the minimum information required to curate city itineraries, save your routes across devices, and provide local transit and dining handoffs.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Identifiers: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic profile identifier, display name, and email address so you can sync saved routes across devices.
  • Saved & Shared Trips: Itineraries you explicitly save or share are stored in Cloud Firestore under your user account and cached locally on your device using Android Room SQLite for offline access.
  • Optional Foreground Location: TripChoice accesses device GPS coordinates only when you explicitly tap the Live GPS button to anchor a route near your current spot. Background location is never collected.
  • Diagnostic & Preference Controls: Optional analytics, crash telemetry, and personalized vibe recommendations can be toggled on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to generate multi-stop city routes tailored to your selected city and vibe, render interactive transit maps and step-by-step directions, cache saved trips for offline travel, and respond to support inquiries sent to us.

3. Third-Party Services & External Links

TripChoice relies on trusted infrastructure providers to deliver core functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Secure sign-in and cloud route synchronization.
  • Google Maps Platform & Gemini API: Map rendering, place grounding, and custom itinerary generation.
  • External Mobility & Dining Links: When you tap external handoff buttons (such as Google Maps directions, Uber, Lyft, GO Taxi, OpenTable, TheFork, or Google Reserve), those third-party apps or websites operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention, Offline Storage & Deletion

Local offline routes remain on your device until you remove them in Saved Trips or uninstall the app. You may delete cloud-synced trips directly inside the app or request complete account and data deletion at any time by emailing support@tripchoice.app.

5. Contact Us

Questions or privacy requests? Reach our team at support@tripchoice.app.
